    The rule underneath. A move outside the bracket acts on y. A move inside acts on x. Moves that act on different letters never interfere, so their order is free. Two translations just add, and two stretches or reflections just multiply, so those pairs are free too.

    Where the order bites. A translation and a stretch or reflection that act on the same letter. Whichever comes second also acts on the first one's result. In an exam, a sequence is marked in the order you write it.
