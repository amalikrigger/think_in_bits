Practice · radians, sectors, segments

Questions 1 to 3. Exact answers where you can, otherwise 3 s.f.

1. (a) Express \(72^\circ\) in radians, in terms of \(\pi\). (b) Express 0.35 radians in degrees.

Tap to check.
(a) \(72 \times \dfrac{\pi}{180} = \dfrac{2\pi}{5}\)    (b) \(0.35 \times \dfrac{180}{\pi} = 20.05\)
\(\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\),  \(20.1^\circ\)

2. A sector has radius 7.5 cm and perimeter 27 cm. Find the angle and the area.

Tap to check.
perimeter = \(2r + r\theta\):  \(15 + 7.5\theta = 27\),  \(\theta = \dfrac{12}{7.5}\)
area:  \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 7.5^2 \times 1.6 = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 56.25 \times 1.6\)
\(\theta = 1.6\) radians,  area \(45\) cm²

3. A sector has radius 10 cm and area 50 cm². Find its angle, its arc length and the area of the segment cut off by its chord.

Tap to check.
angle:  \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 100 \times \theta = 50\),  \(\theta = 1\) radian
arc:  \(10 \times 1 = 10\) cm
segment = sector − triangle:  \(50 - \tfrac{1}{2} \times 100 \times \sin 1 = 50 - 42.074\)
\(\theta = 1\),  arc \(10\) cm,  segment \(7.93\) cm²

Got 49.1 for the segment? Your calculator was in degrees mode for \(\sin 1\).

Practice · perimeters, and working backwards

Questions 4 and 5.

4. A sector has radius 9 cm and angle \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\). Find the exact perimeter of the segment cut off by its chord.

Tap to check.
perimeter of a segment = arc + chord
arc:  \(9 \times \tfrac{\pi}{2} = \tfrac{9\pi}{2}\)
chord:  the angle at the center is a right angle, so by Pythagoras \(\sqrt{9^2 + 9^2} = 9\sqrt{2}\)
\(\dfrac{9\pi}{2} + 9\sqrt{2} \approx 26.9\) cm

5. A sector has perimeter 20 cm and area 16 cm². Find \(r\) and \(\theta\).

Tap to check.
two equations:  \(2r + r\theta = 20\) and \(\tfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta = 16\)
\(r\theta = 20 - 2r\), substitute:  \(\tfrac{1}{2}r(20 - 2r) = 16\),  \(10r - r^2 = 16\)
\(r^2 - 10r + 16 = 0\),  \((r - 2)(r - 8) = 0\)
check each:  \(r = 2\) gives \(\theta = 8\), more than a full turn (\(2\pi \approx 6.28\)): reject.  \(r = 8\) gives \(\theta = \tfrac{4}{8}\).
\(r = 8\) cm,  \(\theta = 0.5\) radians

Practice · compound shapes

Questions 6 and 7. Name the shapes first, then add or subtract.

6. Two sectors share the same center and the same angle, 1.3 radians. One has radius 9 cm, the other 5 cm. Find the area and the perimeter of the region between their arcs.

Tap to check.
area = big sector − small sector:  \(\tfrac{1}{2}(81 - 25)(1.3)\)
perimeter: two arcs and two straight ends, each \(9 - 5 = 4\):
\(9(1.3) + 5(1.3) + 4 + 4 = 11.7 + 6.5 + 8\)
area \(36.4\) cm²,  perimeter \(26.2\) cm

7. Triangle \(ABC\) has \(AB = AC = 6\) cm and angle \(BAC = 0.7\) radians. A sector of a circle with center \(A\) and radius 4 cm is cut out of the triangle at \(A\). Find the area that is left.

Tap to check.
triangle (\(\tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C\), angle between the two 6 cm sides):  \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 \times \sin 0.7 = 11.596\)
sector:  \(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 4^2 \times 0.7 = 5.6\)
\(11.596 - 5.6 = 6.00\) cm²
