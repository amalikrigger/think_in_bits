        Which form does the question want? When a question says "in the form ax + by + c = 0", the coefficients must be whole numbers. Leaving a fraction in costs the mark, so clear the fractions first and then move everything to one side.

        Perpendicular gradients. Two lines are perpendicular when their gradients multiply to −1. Flip the fraction and change the sign: 2/3 becomes −3/2. A horizontal line and a vertical line are perpendicular too, even though the vertical one has no gradient.
