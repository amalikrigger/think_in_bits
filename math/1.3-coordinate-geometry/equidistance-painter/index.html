Why the green points make a circle

Call any green point P(x, y). Its distance from C is r. Write that distance with the distance formula, then square both sides.

Make your prediction, paint some points, then tap Reveal the circle. The working for your C and r appears here.

One idea, two answers. Both tests start from the distance formula, and both times you square it.

With A and B there is a distance on each side of the equation. Each side has its own x² and y², so they cancel. Only x and y terms are left, and that is a straight line.

With C there is just one distance, so nothing cancels. The x² + y² stays in the equation, and that is why you get a circle: (x − a)² + (y − b)² = r².
